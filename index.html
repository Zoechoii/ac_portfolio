about / contact

booking worldwide

AC (dot robot) is a London-based Asian queer DJ and producer blending hardcore, cunty techno, and sexy trance into fast, emotionally charged sets built for pure release. With a background in songwriting, she brings melody and colour into high-intensity club sounds, shaping a sound that’s distinctly her own.

Known for her striking visual identity and immersive performances, she turns every set into a shared, high-energy experience. Since launching her DJ career in 2024, AC (dot robot) has quickly built a reputation for igniting every dance floor she has touched. She has performed at London staples such as Village Underground, E1, Colour Factory, MOT, The Cause, Egg London, XOYO. Brought the heat to SXSW and FKA twigs' album release party; played for Wraith club, Planet Fun, Nitecawr, and collaborated both as a DJ and model with the alternative Asian subculture collective Eastern Margins etc. In 2025, she embarked on a four-city tour across China and South Korea as both a DJ and one of the tour organizers.

As a producer, AC (dot robot) has collaborated with Adhesive record label, an underground electronic label celebrated for its forward-thinking vision and uncompromising edge. She released her EP SoundCloudy and the single NLI-Riot [AC (dot robot) Remix], which premiered via MOTZ Berlin Mag. Her new music “London 2026 - EP” has featured on Magnetic Magazine, Kaltblut Magazine.
